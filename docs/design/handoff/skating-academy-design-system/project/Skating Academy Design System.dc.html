Skating Academy Design System

Mobile-first tokens and components for three audiences: academy admins on desktop, coaches on a phone rink-side with gloves on, and parents checking a skater's progress. Light mode only.

Decisions I made for you
  1. Primary action is ink, not red. On the attendance screen red means absent and green means present. A red primary button next to those would misread, so brand red is reserved for identity, destructive actions and the absent state.
  2. Corners are rounded (4–16px). A deliberate departure from the parent Modernist system's 0px radius — a sports app a parent reads in ten seconds wants softer affordances. Everything else stays Modernist: Archivo, flush-left labels, 2px section rules.
  3. Minimum control height is 44px; 56px rink-side. Coach-facing controls step up a size class rather than getting their own component.
Open questions for you
  1. Does the academy have existing brand colors this should defer to? The palette currently inherits the attached design system's red.
  2. Is Archivo acceptable on Android and iOS, or do you want a system-font fallback path for the native shells?
  3. Should parents and coaches share one component set, or do you want a separate coach-only "rink" size scale documented as its own variant?

07

Rink-side rules

Gloves, one hand, low winter sun off the ice. These override any other preference in the system.

Attendance row — 72px, whole row is the target
{{ attendanceRows }}

Tap the row to cycle present → absent → late → unset. Two 56px buttons per row, no gap smaller than 8px, and the state is carried by fill, label and position — never colour alone.

Non-negotiables
  1. Every tap target ≥44×44px; coach-facing primary actions ≥56px.
  2. Body text never below 16px on mobile; no text on a photo without an ink scrim.
  3. Text contrast ≥4.5:1 against its own background — that rules out neutral-500 on white for anything but decoration.
  4. Interactive borders are 1.5px, not hairlines: 1px vanishes in glare.
  5. No colour-only state. Attendance, fees and level all carry a word or icon.
  6. Nothing important in the top 88px — that's out of one-handed thumb reach.
  7. Destructive actions confirm; everything else offers Undo in a toast instead.
  8. Every write works offline and reconciles later; show the offline toast, not a blocking error.
